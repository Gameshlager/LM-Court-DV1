Our Courts

Leila Meskhi Tennis Courts are an important place for tennis in Georgia. They are named after Leila Meskhi, a famous Georgian tennis player who represented her country in international competitions. The courts provide a place for people of different ages to practise tennis, improve their skills and enjoy sports. They also help young players develop their talent and become professional athletes. The academy is located on Kote Marjanishvili Street in Tbilisi. It has indoor and outdoor tennis courts where players can train throughout the year. It also hosts local and international tennis tournaments. One of its goals is to make tennis more popular in Georgia and help young people become successful athletes. Leila Meskhi's Tennis Academy is an important part of Georgian sports history and continues to support the development of tennis in the country.
